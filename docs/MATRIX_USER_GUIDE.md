🚀 First-Time Account Setup (Onboarding)
New users must activate their accounts before their first login:

* **Visit the Setup Link:** Go to [https://matrix.acoblighting.com/auth/setup-account](https://matrix.acoblighting.com/auth/setup-account).
* **Enter Company Email:** Input your official company email address. The system exclusively accepts emails ending with:
  * `@org.acoblighting.com`
  * `@acoblighting.com`
* **Request Code:** Click **Set Up**. The system will automatically generate and send a 6-digit verification code to your email.
* **Verify Account:** Enter the 6-digit code on the setup screen and click **Verify and Continue**.
* **Set Password:** Enter and confirm your new secure password in the password fields.
* **Log In:** After saving your password, you will be redirected to the login screen at [https://matrix.acoblighting.com/auth/login](https://matrix.acoblighting.com/auth/login). Enter your email and new password to log in.
